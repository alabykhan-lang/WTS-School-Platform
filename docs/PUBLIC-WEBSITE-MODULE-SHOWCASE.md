# Public Website: Module Showcase

## Public entry rule

The public website exposes one staff entry: **WTS Staff Workspace**. The header, mobile navigation, homepage platform section and footer all point to that same named entrance.

The public `/portal` page is a showcase and orientation page. Its only protected call to action is the WTS Staff Workspace sign-in. It does not expose specialist login choices, technical deployment URLs, internal routes, database wording or authentication details.

## Showcase cards

The public showcase describes:

- Central Registry — operational foundation for identity, pupil records and access;
- Results — operational academic record and report-card service;
- Attendance — coming online, with no invented attendance figures;
- Notifications — coming online, with no invented delivery figures;
- Reports — planned; and
- future school services — planned.

Each card contains a module name, short description, school-community benefit, visual mark and honest operational status. Cards are informational and do not grant access or expose school records.

## Standalone services

Standalone specialist URLs remain operational and independently protected. They are not listed as public login destinations. Authenticated Workspace launch links may open the appropriate service only after the Workspace has received a real active grant.
